Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026

RoamDial (roamdial.com) plays live radio from around the world. There are no accounts and no ads, and we don't sell data.

Stored only in your browser

Saved stations and songs, language, volume, the last station you heard and your analytics choice are kept in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes them.

Streams and other services

Audio comes directly from each station's own server, not through ours. Lyrics and similar features are looked up by your browser from third-party services. These servers see your IP address, and their own policies apply.

Anonymous usage and error reports

To improve RoamDial and fix playback problems, we collect anonymous usage and error information: which features are used, which stations fail to play, browser type and approximate country. We don't store your IP address — only a code derived from it with a secret key, so we can tell visitors apart and stop abuse. Usage records are kept for 90 days. None of this is used for advertising or sold.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how RoamDial is used: pages viewed, a few actions (start listening, next station, playback failed, save, share), approximate location and device/browser type. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses. Event data is kept for 2 months. Advertising features are off.

In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics loads only after you allow it.

Complaints & contact

If you run a station or hold rights and want something removed, or have any other complaint, email [email protected] and we'll deal with it promptly.

For privacy questions, email [email protected].

隐私说明

更新于 2026-10-16

RoamDial（roamdial.com）播放世界各地的电台直播。没有账号，没有广告，不出售任何数据。

只存在你的浏览器里

收藏的台和歌、语言、音量、上次听的台，以及你对统计的选择，都存在浏览器本地。清除本站数据即可删除。

电台音频与其他服务

声音直接来自各电台自己的服务器，不经过我们；歌词等功能由你的浏览器直接向第三方服务查询。这些服务器能看到你的 IP 地址，适用对方自己的政策。

匿名使用与错误统计

为了改进产品、排查播放问题，我们会匿名收集使用情况和错误信息：用了哪些功能、哪些台播放失败、浏览器类型和大致国家。我们不保存你的 IP 地址，只保存用密钥由它算出的代号，用来区分不同访客、防止刷量。使用记录保留 90 天。这些信息不用于广告，也不出售。

Google Analytics

我们用 Google Analytics 4 了解大家怎么使用 RoamDial：看了哪些页面、几个操作（开始收听、下一站、播放失败、收藏、分享）、大致位置、设备和浏览器类型。Google Analytics 4 不保存完整 IP 地址。事件数据保留 2 个月。广告功能关闭。

在欧盟 / 欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士，你同意之后才会加载 Google Analytics。
